A web-based 3D repository built from materials developed during Land Arts of the American West's 2020 *Adaptation* program. It began as an interpretation of the historical meanders of the Rio Grande / Río Bravo, where the river forms the US–Mexico border, within a larger effort with Texas Tech University, Marfa Books, Zoe Leonard Studio and a wide research network ([original prospectus](https://landarts.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/2020-Land-Arts-ADAPTATION-prospectus.pdf)).

The site is an interactive, user-contributed database of stories from the river. Each story is placed where it was told, against the river's shifting channels, and the underlying geographic data is published as open GeoJSON.
