Invited by the artists Elizabeth Ogilvie and Robert Page, I served as monitor and ethnographer for *Into the Oceanic* during its unveiling at COP26, organized with the Glasgow School of Art. The project merges art, science and activism to put the oceans' role in sustaining life at the centre of the climate summit, through immersive installations, projections and workshops.

My role was to document its week-long run and the public conversation it opened around marine health, and to lead an ethnography workshop alongside it.
