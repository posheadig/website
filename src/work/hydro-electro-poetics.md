A body of practice-based research developing kinetic models that combine analog and digital components.

## Hydrorefractor, 2019

A mechanism propelled by computer cooling, producing diffracting light patterns and the kinetic movement of water.

{% vimeo "699778827?h=7a1b56a50e", "Hydrorefractor." %}

## Earlier studies

{% vimeo "244088261?h=485a3a4732", "River Orchestra Machine #1.", "480 / 520" %}

*River mobile* (2017) is propelled by the river itself and amplifies the friction of its movement as sound. A 2018 prototype developed the mechanisms later used in Hydrorefractor.

{% vimeo "300312857?h=f1550e9dd1", "Marine Magnetism, 2018 — a leaf floating on water, after the relationship between tides, the magnetic field and rising heat.", "9 / 16" %}
