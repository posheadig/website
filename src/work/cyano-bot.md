Exploratory research into robotics in Lake Champlain, where the robot embodies a blob of cyanobacteria. Built from a microcontroller and micro water pumps, the autonomous body propels itself in search of the brightest sunlight.

Its movement is streamed into a 3D environment program, which projects the robot's photosensor data into a cityscape after sea levels have risen to the point where the habitat has become ideal for cyanobacteria.
